# Dashboard stories

Both narratives use the reconciled city-category extract. Network totals are 600 bookings, revenue ₹10,47,973, and 79 SLA breaches (13.2%).

## City Ops lead

**Headline.** Bengaluru has the highest SLA breach rate, at 15.9%.

**Evidence.** Bengaluru recorded 17 SLA breaches on 107 bookings, a breach rate of 15.9%. That is 8.7 percentage points above Delhi NCR, the lowest city, at 7.2% (6 breaches on 83 bookings). The network rate on the same reconciled extract is 13.2% (79 breaches / 600 bookings). City revenue on this extract is ₹1,79,835 for Bengaluru and ₹1,40,771 for Delhi NCR.

**Implication.** The City Ops lead for Bengaluru should treat missed SLAs as the first operating problem this period: review the breached jobs before adding capacity, and do not read the city's revenue figure as evidence that delivery is under control. Delhi NCR is the comparison point for what the breach rate looks like when the same rules are met more often.

## Category lead

**Headline.** Deep Home Cleaning is the strongest category by revenue, and Salon for Women is the comparatively under-booked category.

**Evidence.** Deep Home Cleaning produced ₹5,08,964 from 176 bookings, equal to 48.6% of network revenue of ₹10,47,973. Salon for Women produced ₹1,51,689 from 65 bookings, the lowest booking count of the six active categories. Full category ranking by revenue:

- Deep Home Cleaning: revenue ₹5,08,964, bookings 176, SLA breaches 21
- Salon for Women: revenue ₹1,51,689, bookings 65, SLA breaches 7
- Electrical Repair: revenue ₹1,19,881, bookings 112, SLA breaches 13
- AC Repair & Service: revenue ₹1,13,039, bookings 74, SLA breaches 11
- Plumbing: revenue ₹80,837, bookings 95, SLA breaches 13
- Salon for Men: revenue ₹73,563, bookings 78, SLA breaches 14

**Implication.** The Category lead should protect supply and partner quality in Deep Home Cleaning, because that is where the rupee volume sits. Salon for Women needs a demand check — partner coverage, price band, and city mix — before it is treated as a smaller business by design. Booking count, not revenue alone, is what marks it as under-booked.
